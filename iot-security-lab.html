How to use this lab

You are auditing a small network of simulated smart devices. Everything happens in the terminal; nothing real is attacked.

  1. scan finds the devices. Watch them light up on the bench.
  2. connect <ip> picks a device, or open a device and use Connect in terminal.
  3. enum lists its open ports. vuln-scan lists its weaknesses (v1, v2, ...).
  4. exploit <id> safely shows the risk. patch <id> or patch-all fixes it.
  5. Finish all six objectives, enter your name and click Finish Lab.

Dome light colors

Grey: not found yet
Blue: found by scan
Amber: weaknesses found
Red: compromised
Green: fully patched

The dials, lenses and keypads on the devices are decoration only. Press Esc to close a close-up or this box.
