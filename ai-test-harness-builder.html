AI Test Harness Builder

An AI can run a scan in seconds — and just as fast it will scan a host you weren't hired to test, launch a denial-of-service, or report a vulnerability it never verified. A prompt that says “please stay in scope” cannot stop it. A test harness is the framework that wraps the AI so it can help safely: it holds the engagement's authorization, enforces a policy on every operation the AI proposes, and logs every decision. The rule it runs on: the AI proposes, the harness governs, the human validates. In this lab you build the governing core of a harness for a scanning and vulnerability-testing engagement.
How this works: Work the three stages on the right. In Stage 1 you authorize the engagement; in Stage 2 you build the policy engine; in Stage 3 you run your harness against the operations the AI proposes and watch it govern them. Each decision has free Background and Hint help. Finding validation — the “human validates” part — is the Week 5 lab. Auto-graded; screenshot the Finish & Grade summary.
