Join — least upper bound

The lowest label that dominates both. A subject needs at least this clearance to read A and B.

Meet — greatest lower bound

The highest label both A and B dominate. Anything at this label is readable by subjects at A and at B.

Common upper bounds — the join is the lowest of these
Common lower bounds — the meet is the highest of these

Every pair of the 16 labels has exactly one join and one meet. That property is what makes this partial order a lattice. Its top, (TS, {Army, Navy}), is system high; its bottom, (U, { }), is system low.
